Rules & Regulations (Season 10 • T12 Format)

Official Digital Rulebook

Looking to review complete match protocols, tournament bylaws, and disciplinary standards?

Open Rulebook

• Season 10 will strictly follow official T12 format (12 overs per innings) on 15/11/26 (Sunday).

• Strict Bowling Limit: Each bowler can bowl a maximum of 2 overs only throughout the entire innings.

• The tournament will be contested as a 3-match series played consecutively (back-to-back) on the same match day at L . Park Ground.

• The entire tournament will be strictly conducted using official i10 balls.

• Teams are completely responsible for carrying their own playing equipment, specifically cricket bats. Match-ready i10 will be continuously supplied by the on-ground organizing body.

• Player registry is open across 34 tournament slots with active players confirmed per squad.

• Only registered players confirmed on the official list are eligible to participate in the matches.

• Any player substitutions during the match require prior inspection and approval from central organizers.

NRR Math Formulation: [Total Runs Scored / Total Overs Faced] - [Total Runs Conceded / Total Overs Bowled]

• Critical Caveat: If a batting team loses all wickets before batting out their assigned quota, the calculator treats the inning as completing the full assigned 12 overs structurally (applied overs).

• The field umpire's decision is completely absolute, final, and binding under all circumstances.

• Any physical/verbal altercation or behavior undermining match officials triggers negative deduction penalties directly affecting your standings on the Best Discipline Leaderboard and carries risks of total team disqualification.
