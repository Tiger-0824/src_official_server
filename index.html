Rules & Regulations

Official Digital Rulebook

Looking to review complete match protocols, tournament bylaws, and disciplinary standards?

Open Rulebook

• Every match in this championship series strictly consists of 30 overs per innings.

• A maximum bowling constraint applies: strictly 6 overs maximum per bowler in an innings.

• The tournament will be contested across a 4-match series (27th Sep, 4th Oct, 11th Oct, 18th Oct), with a 5th tie-breaker decider match scheduled on 25th Oct if the series ties 2–2.

• The entire tournament will be strictly conducted using official i10 balls.

• Teams are completely responsible for carrying their own playing equipment, specifically cricket bats. Match-ready i10 will be continuously supplied by the on-ground organizing body.

• The tournament completely locks registration across the official registry pool.

• Each playing squad fields 9 active players per match.

• Absolutely no outside wildcard player entries are permitted once match schedules commence. Substitute fielders are authorized under strict physical injury assessment, entirely subject to the central organisers green light.

NRR Math Formulation: [Total Runs Scored / Total Overs Faced] - [Total Runs Conceded / Total Overs Bowled]

• Critical Caveat: If a batting team loses all wickets before batting out their assigned quota, the calculator treats the inning as completing the full assigned quota structurally (applied overs).

• The field umpire's decision is completely absolute, final, and binding under all circumstances.

• Any physical/verbal altercation or behavior undermining match officials triggers negative deduction penalties directly affecting your standings on the Best Discipline Leaderboard and carries risks of total team disqualification.
