Rules & Regulations (Season 11 • ODI Format)

Official Digital Rulebook

Looking to review complete match protocols, tournament bylaws, and disciplinary standards?

Open Rulebook

• Season 11 of Samal Rout Cup will strictly follow official ODI cricket format rules & regulations.

• The tournament is structured as a 3-match ODI bilateral series between Legions XI and Rourkela Yodhas across three consecutive Sundays: 22nd Nov, 29th Nov, and 6th Dec at L . Park Ground.

• Innings pacing, bowler over-quotas, fielding restrictions, and powerplay rules conform strictly to organizers' ODI standards.

• The entire tournament will be strictly conducted using official i10 balls.

• Teams are completely responsible for carrying their own playing equipment, specifically cricket bats. Match-ready i10 balls will be supplied by the on-ground organizing body.

• Exactly 20 registered players are confirmed for Season 11 across Legions XI and Rourkela Yodhas (10 players per squad).

• Only registered players confirmed on the official squad list are eligible to participate in the ODI fixtures.

• Any player substitutions during a match require prior inspection and approval from central organizers.

NRR Math Formulation: [Total Runs Scored / Total Overs Faced] - [Total Runs Conceded / Total Overs Bowled]

• Critical Caveat: If a batting team loses all wickets before batting out their assigned ODI over quota, the calculator treats the inning as completing the full assigned quota structurally (applied overs).

• The field umpire's decision is completely absolute, final, and binding under all circumstances.

• Any physical/verbal altercation or behavior undermining match officials triggers negative deduction penalties directly affecting your standings on the Fair Play Discipline Leaderboard and carries risks of total team disqualification.
